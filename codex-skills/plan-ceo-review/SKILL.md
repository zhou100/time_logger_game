# plan-ceo-review

Use this skill to evaluate a strategy/plan document before execution.

## Checklist
1. Problem framing: Is the user/problem/goal crisp?
2. Decision quality: Are tradeoffs explicit?
3. Execution fit: Are owner, sequence, and dependencies clear?
4. Measurement: Are leading + lagging metrics defined?
5. Rollout safety: Are guardrails and go/no-go criteria present?

## Output format
- Strengths (3-5 bullets)
- Gaps / risks (prioritized)
- Concrete edits (copy-ready)
- Go/No-Go recommendation with conditions

## Scoring (1-5)
- Strategic clarity
- Execution readiness
- Measurement quality
- Risk containment

Any score <=2 must include a blocking action.
